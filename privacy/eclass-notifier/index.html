1. What the App does

eClass Notifier helps students using the University of Athens (UoA) eClass platform stay informed about new announcements, messages, and calendar events posted by their professors.

Users provide their eClass login credentials to connect the App to their university account. The App retrieves relevant information from eClass, stores it to provide a convenient interface for viewing announcements, messages, and calendar events, and sends push notifications when new information is detected.

The App also provides graphical interfaces for viewing this information, including a calendar view of academic events.

eClass Notifier is an independent third-party application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially associated with the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens or its eClass platform.

2. Summary

  • We process your eClass login credentials, academic information retrieved from your eClass account, a Firebase user identifier, and a push-notification token.
  • eClass credentials are stored locally using Flutter Secure Storage and in encrypted form on Firebase Cloud.
  • Google Cloud Key Management Service (KMS) is used to protect credentials stored in the cloud.
  • We do not collect your location, camera, photos, microphone, contacts, health, biometric, or financial data.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking services.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • You can delete your App data directly through the App.

3. Information we collect

3.1 eClass account credentials

To use the App, you must provide the username and password associated with your University of Athens eClass account.

These credentials are stored locally on your device using Flutter Secure Storage. They are also transmitted to our backend through secure connections and stored in encrypted form in Firebase Cloud.

We use Google Cloud Key Management Service (KMS) encryption to protect credentials stored in the cloud. Our backend services can decrypt and use these credentials when necessary to authenticate with eClass and retrieve your academic information.

Your eClass credentials are used solely to provide the App's functionality. We do not use them to access your university account for unrelated purposes.

3.2 Account information

The App uses Firebase Authentication to identify users and manage their sessions. A unique user identifier is generated and associated with the user's App data.

The App does not require or store your personal name or email address as part of its Firebase user account. Authentication uses Firebase custom authentication tokens associated with a user identifier.

3.3 Academic information

To provide its functionality, the App retrieves and stores information available through your eClass account. This may include:

  • Full announcement titles and content;
  • Full message titles and content, including associated links and text;
  • Names of professors or other senders associated with announcements and messages;
  • Course identifiers associated with announcements, messages, and events;
  • Calendar event titles;
  • Calendar event start and end dates and times;
  • Calendar event types;
  • Optional calendar event descriptions or content;
  • Additional calendar event information provided by eClass.

The App does not download or store attachments or files associated with announcements or messages.

This information is retrieved from your own eClass account and stored in Firebase to allow the App to display it and detect new information.

3.4 Push notifications

If notifications are enabled, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) provides us with a device registration token and associated platform information. We use this token to deliver push notifications to your device.

On Apple devices, Firebase Cloud Messaging manages delivery through Apple's Push Notification service (APNs).

Notifications contain generic information about the number of new items detected, such as:

  • The number of new announcements;
  • The number of new messages;
  • The number of new calendar events.

Notifications do not contain the actual content of announcements, messages, or calendar events, nor do they include their full titles or descriptions.

The App also provides a notification frequency preference that can limit notifications to once every 10 hours. You can disable push notifications through your device settings at any time.

3.5 App configuration and technical information

The App stores configuration information required to operate its services. This includes scraping configurations, notification preferences, and information associated with your device's push-notification registration.

The App uses Firebase services, including Firebase App Check, to help protect its backend services and verify legitimate App requests.

Basic technical information, such as app version, platform information, and diagnostic information generated by the Firebase SDKs, may be processed as necessary to operate and secure the services.

3.6 What we do not collect

The App does not request or intentionally collect:

  • Precise or approximate location;
  • Camera, photos, or media files;
  • Microphone or audio;
  • Contacts;
  • Health or biometric information;
  • Financial or payment information;
  • Advertising identifiers or advertising data;
  • Information about other students beyond information made available through your own eClass account;
  • Attachments or files associated with eClass announcements or messages.

We do not use advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

We share or process information only through the service providers necessary to operate the App:

  • Google (Firebase / Google Cloud) — Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore database storage, Firebase Cloud Functions, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Firebase App Check, and Google Cloud Key Management Service (KMS) for encryption key management. Google acts as our service provider and data processor for these services.
  • Apple Inc. — Apple's Push Notification service (APNs) is used to deliver push notifications to Apple devices through Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • University of Athens eClass platform — the App connects to the eClass platform using the credentials you provide in order to retrieve your academic information. The university platform processes your information under its own privacy policy and terms.

Our Firebase Cloud Functions use your credentials to authenticate with eClass and retrieve the information required by the App. The credentials are not provided to unrelated third parties.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or when necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the public.

7. Retention

We retain your personal data and academic information only for as long as necessary to provide the App's functionality.

Your eClass credentials, academic information, notification preferences, device registration information, and other App configuration data are stored in Firebase while your App account remains active.

We do not intentionally retain academic information after you use the App's data deletion function. Information is removed from the App's Firebase storage when the deletion operation is performed successfully.

Limited information may remain where retention is required by applicable law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

8. Account and data deletion

You can delete your App data directly from within eClass Notifier using the dedicated data deletion function.

When you activate this function:

  • All locally stored App data and credentials are deleted from your device;
  • Your encrypted eClass credentials stored in Firebase are deleted;
  • Your stored announcements and their content are deleted;
  • Your stored messages and their content are deleted;
  • Your stored calendar events are deleted;
  • Your scraping configurations and associated user data are deleted;
  • Your notification preferences and stored FCM registration information are deleted;
  • Your associated Firebase user data is deleted.

Deleting your App data stops the App's synchronization and scraping processes because the information and configurations required to perform them are removed.

Important: This deletion affects only eClass Notifier and the data stored by its services. It does not delete your University of Athens account or any information stored on the university's eClass platform.

The Firebase Authentication entry associated with your user identifier is not explicitly deleted by this process. It may remain in Firebase Authentication without the associated App data. This remaining authentication entry is not used to retain your academic information or credentials.

The deletion function is designed to remove your associated App data immediately when the operation succeeds. Information that must be retained by law will be kept only for the period required by law.

11. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through eClass Notifier.

These measures include:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for communication between the App and backend services;
  • Secure local credential storage using Flutter Secure Storage;

Access to encrypted credentials is restricted to the backend processes that require them to authenticate with eClass and retrieve academic information.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Although we take reasonable precautions to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
