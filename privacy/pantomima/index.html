1. What the App does

Παντομίμα is a party game application that allows users to play pantomime games in teams or in free-play mode. The App provides built-in words and phrases for players to act out and allows users to configure game settings, such as round duration and difficulty preferences.

The App operates entirely on the user's device. Its game content is bundled with the application in a built-in JSON file. No internet connection or online service is required for the App's functionality.

2. Summary

  • The App does not collect personal information.
  • No account creation or sign-in is required.
  • The App does not use a backend or transmit information over the internet.
  • The App does not request device permissions.
  • The App does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking services.
  • The App does not contain advertisements.
  • Game preferences are stored locally on your device using Flutter SharedPreferences.
  • All game words and phrases are included in the application. Users cannot create or upload their own words.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Personal information

We do not collect, request, or store personal information about users. The App does not require an account, username, email address, telephone number, or any other personal identifier to function.

3.2 Game settings and preferences

The App stores certain game settings locally on your device using Flutter SharedPreferences. These settings include:

  • Sound enabled or disabled;
  • Vibration enabled or disabled;
  • Theme preference (light, dark, or system);
  • Default round duration;
  • Preferred game difficulties;
  • Streak bonus enabled or disabled.

This information is stored exclusively on your device. It is not transmitted to SymplyApps or any external server. We do not have access to these settings.

3.3 Built-in game content

The words and phrases used during gameplay are included within the App in a built-in JSON file. They are bundled with the application and are accessed locally.

Users cannot create, upload, or submit their own words or phrases through the App.

3.4 Device information and permissions

The App does not request device permissions and does not intentionally collect device identifiers, location data, contacts, photos, camera data, microphone recordings, or other personal device information.

The App does not use online services to transmit user information or gameplay data.

3.5 What we do not collect

The App does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact details;
  • Precise or approximate location;
  • Camera, photos, or media files;
  • Microphone recordings or audio;
  • Contacts;
  • Health, biometric, or financial information;
  • Advertising identifiers or advertising data;
  • Analytics, tracking, or user behavior data;
  • Game history or personal gameplay profiles.

4. How we use information and our legal bases

Since the App does not collect or transmit personal information, we do not process personal data for advertising, profiling, analytics, or marketing purposes.

The limited settings stored locally on your device are used solely to remember your preferences and provide the expected gameplay experience.

Where applicable, the local storage of these preferences is necessary to provide the functionality requested by the user.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information with third parties.

The App does not connect to any backend, cloud service, or external server. It does not use third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, or tracking.

All game functionality and settings operate locally on your device. No personal information or gameplay data is transmitted to SymplyApps or other parties through the App.

6. International transfers

The App does not transmit personal information to external servers or third-party services.

Since no personal information is collected or transferred by the App, there are no international data transfers associated with its normal operation.

7. Retention

The App stores game preferences locally on your device using Flutter SharedPreferences.

These preferences remain on your device until they are cleared through your device's application data settings or the App is uninstalled.

SymplyApps does not maintain copies of these settings on external servers and does not have access to them.

The built-in game words and phrases are part of the application itself and are not user data.

8. Data deletion

The App does not create user accounts or maintain personal information on external servers. Therefore, there is no account deletion process or server-side data deletion request required.

You can remove the locally stored game preferences by clearing the App's data through your device settings.

Uninstalling the App generally removes its locally stored application data, including the saved game preferences.

The App does not retain copies of your settings or gameplay information after they are removed from your device.

We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date and using the security features provided by your device manufacturer.
