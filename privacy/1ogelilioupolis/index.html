1. What the App does

The App provides a simple way to access the public website of 1ο ΓΕΛ Ηλιούπολης: https://1lyk-ilioup.att.sch.gr/.

The App displays a loading screen while the website loads and then presents the website inside an Android WebView. Its only additional functionality relates to navigation within the WebView, including Android back-button handling.

The App does not provide its own user accounts, messaging system, content submission functionality, or other online services. It acts as a web-viewing interface for the school's website.

2. Summary

  • The App does not require registration or sign-in.
  • SymplyApps does not collect or maintain personal information through the App.
  • The App does not have its own backend or database.
  • The App does not use Firebase, analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • The App does not display advertisements.
  • The App does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos.
  • The App does not provide file-download functionality.
  • The App loads the school's website, which may independently process technical information when you visit it.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information collected by SymplyApps

SymplyApps does not intentionally collect, request, or store personal information through the App.

The App does not ask users to provide names, email addresses, telephone numbers, account credentials, or other personal details.

We do not operate a server or database that receives and stores users' browsing activity or information through the App.

3.2 Website information and processing

The App loads content directly from the school's website. When you access the website through the WebView, your device communicates with the website and its hosting infrastructure.

The website and its hosting provider may independently process technical information associated with website requests, such as IP addresses, browser or device information, request timestamps, and server logs. The website may also use cookies or similar technologies according to its own configuration.

Such processing is performed by the operators of the website and its hosting infrastructure, not by SymplyApps as part of the App's own data collection.

3.3 App storage

The App does not maintain its own user profiles, preferences, browsing history, or application database. It does not intentionally save website content or user-submitted information.

Any website-related data handled internally by the Android WebView or the website itself is subject to the relevant Android and website behavior and policies.

3.4 Device permissions and technical access

The App does not request runtime access to sensitive device features such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos.

The App requires network connectivity to load and display the school's website. Android may therefore use the standard Internet permission required for WebView network access. This is a normal Android permission and does not display a runtime permission prompt to the user.

4. How we use information and our legal bases

SymplyApps does not collect or process personal information through the App for advertising, analytics, profiling, or marketing purposes.

The App uses an internet connection solely to retrieve and display the content of the school's website.

Any personal data processing associated with requests to the school's website is carried out independently by the website operators and its hosting provider, under their applicable privacy practices and legal obligations.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information collected through the App with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

The App does not transmit user information to SymplyApps or to any backend operated by us.

When you use the App, your device connects directly to the school's website: 1lyk-ilioup.att.sch.gr. The website and its hosting provider may receive technical information necessary to deliver the requested pages and operate their services.

The school website may contain links to external websites. If you follow such links, you will be accessing services operated by other parties. Those services may have their own privacy policies and data-processing practices. SymplyApps does not control those services.

6. International transfers

SymplyApps does not operate servers or backend services that receive or store personal information through the App.

For information about such processing and any international transfers, please consult the school's website privacy information or contact the website operators.

7. Retention

SymplyApps does not maintain a database of users, browsing histories, or personal information collected through the App.

SymplyApps does not control the retention periods of information processed independently by the school's website or its hosting provider.
