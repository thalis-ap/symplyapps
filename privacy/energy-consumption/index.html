1. What the App does

Κατανάλωση ενέργειας helps users estimate how much electricity household appliances and other home devices consume. Users can calculate estimated energy consumption in kilowatt-hours (kWh) and estimate the associated electricity cost.

To display an up-to-date electricity price for Greece, the App retrieves publicly available price information from GlobalPetrolPrices — Greece electricity prices. The request is made synchronously over the internet when the App needs to obtain the price information.

2. Summary

  • The App does not require an account or login.
  • We do not intentionally collect personal information through the App.
  • Appliance values entered by users are processed locally on the device.
  • The App connects to GlobalPetrolPrices to retrieve electricity price information for Greece.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information entered into the App

The App may allow you to enter appliance details or values needed to estimate electricity consumption and cost. These values are used locally on your device to perform calculations. We do not receive or store these entries on our servers.

3.2 Internet request to retrieve electricity prices

The App makes an internet request to the following public webpage to retrieve electricity price information: https://www.globalpetrolprices.com/Greece/electricity_prices/. The request is made synchronously when the App needs to obtain the price information.

As with ordinary internet connections, the website and its hosting or network providers may receive technical connection information such as your device's IP address, request time, and standard request metadata. This information is handled by the website's operators under their own privacy practices. We do not receive or maintain a separate record of your IP address through this request.

3.3 What we do not collect

We do not intentionally collect or access personal information such as your name, email address, account credentials, precise location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data, financial information, or advertising identifiers. The App does not provide user accounts, and we do not operate a user database for this App.

4. How information is used and our legal bases

The App uses locally entered appliance information solely to calculate estimated electricity consumption and costs. It uses the internet request described above solely to retrieve the electricity price displayed in the App.

We do not use information entered into the App to identify users, create profiles, advertise to users, or track activity across apps or websites. To the extent any personal data is processed by the external website through the ordinary internet request, that processing is carried out by the website operator under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data, and we do not provide user-entered appliance information to third parties.

The App connects to:

  • GlobalPetrolPrices — the App requests publicly available electricity price information for Greece from its electricity prices webpage. The website may process technical connection information, including the IP address from which the request originates. Please consult the website's own privacy policy for details of its data handling.

We do not integrate Firebase, advertising networks, analytics services, or other third-party SDKs for the App's functionality, as described to us.

6. International transfers

We do not operate a backend or user database for this App. However, when the App requests the electricity price webpage, the request is handled by GlobalPetrolPrices and its hosting or network providers. Depending on their infrastructure, technical connection information may be processed in countries outside Greece or the European Economic Area.

For information about any international transfers made by the website operator, please consult its privacy policy and terms.

7. Retention

We do not maintain user accounts or store personal information on our own servers through this App. Values entered for consumption calculations are processed locally on your device. Any locally retained settings or cached information remain on your device unless removed by you or by your device's operating system.

Technical request logs, if any, may be retained by GlobalPetrolPrices or its service providers under their own retention policies. We do not control those records.

8. Account and data deletion

The App does not create user accounts and does not maintain a user profile or personal-data record that can be deleted from our servers.

You can remove locally stored App information by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App, using the options provided by your device. This does not delete technical logs that may have been created by GlobalPetrolPrices when the App retrieved electricity price information.

For requests concerning information processed by GlobalPetrolPrices, please contact the website operator directly.
