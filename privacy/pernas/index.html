1. What the App does

Περνάς; Πανελλήνιες Μόρια helps students calculate and understand grades related to the Greek educational system. It helps final-year high-school students estimate their performance and admission points (“μόρια”) for the Panhellenic examinations. It also provides tools that help younger students calculate the grades they need to pass their school year.

The App also provides educational information about university departments, including courses offered in different semesters, admission-score information and graphs, as well as past examination material that users may view or save as PDF files.

The App is an independent educational application. Unless expressly stated otherwise, it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially associated with the Greek Ministry of Education, any university, or any other educational institution. Information and calculations are provided for informational and educational purposes and should be checked against official sources where necessary.

2. Summary

  • The App does not require users to create an account or provide a name, email address, password, or other profile information.
  • Firebase Anonymous Authentication is used to provide access to the App's database. It creates a technical anonymous user identifier, not a personal profile.
  • Educational information, such as school and university data, is retrieved from Firebase and may be cached locally on your device for faster operation.
  • A device identifier is accessed and sent to our Firebase backend only when you choose to participate in an App competition. A salted representation is stored for competition-related functionality.
  • Competition-related records may be retained so that the App can administer competitions and display information about the latest winner.
  • The App displays banner and interstitial advertisements through Google AdMob. Google may process device and advertising information to provide and measure advertisements.
  • We do not sell personal data. We do not use the competition identifier for advertising or unrelated purposes.
  • The App does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or existing files.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Anonymous authentication information

The App uses Firebase Authentication with anonymous authentication. This creates a unique technical user identifier so that the App can access its Firebase-backed services.

Anonymous authentication does not require you to provide your name, email address, telephone number, username, or password. We do not use this identifier to create a personal profile or to identify you by name.

The anonymous authentication identifier is technical information associated with the App's Firebase access. It is processed by Firebase Authentication and may be used by the App's backend services as necessary to provide and protect the App's functionality.

3.2 Educational information and locally stored data

The App retrieves educational information from our Firebase database to provide its features. This information may include:

  • School and educational information used by the App's grade calculators;
  • University and university-department information;
  • Course names and courses organized by academic semester;
  • Admission-score (“μόρια”) information and related historical data;
  • Graphs and other educational statistics;
  • Past Panhellenic examination topics and related PDF documents;
  • Other educational content made available through the App.

This information is general educational content and is not submitted by users as personal information. The App may cache database content and other non-personal App data locally on your device, including JSON data files, to improve loading speed and provide its core functionality.

The App may allow you to save PDF examination documents to your device. This happens at your request. The App does not request general access to your existing documents or files for this purpose. Locally cached content and saved educational documents are not associated with a personal user profile by us.

3.3 Competition information and device identifier

The App may organize competitions or giveaways. A device identifier (UUID) is accessed and transmitted to our Firebase backend only when you choose to participate in a competition. The App does not use this competition identifier for its ordinary educational features.

When the identifier reaches our backend, a Firebase Cloud Function processes it using a randomly generated salt. A salted representation of the identifier is stored for competition-related purposes, such as distinguishing participants and recognizing a returning participant in a later interaction.

We may retain the competition-related information needed to operate the competition and to display information about the most recent winner. This may include the salted participant identifier and records relating to participation and the competition result. We do not use this information for advertising or unrelated profiling.

The device identifier is a pseudonymous identifier. Although we do not use it to identify you by your name, it may still be treated as personal data under applicable data protection law.

3.4 Advertising and advertising-related information

The App displays banner advertisements and interstitial (full-screen) advertisements using the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google AdMob.

Google and its advertising partners may process information in connection with providing, selecting, measuring, and improving advertisements. Depending on the device, applicable settings, consent choices, and the advertising services used, this information may include:

  • IP address and information derived from it, such as approximate location;
  • Device identifiers, including advertising identifiers where available and permitted;
  • Device type, operating system, app version, and technical information;
  • Information about advertisements displayed, viewed, or interacted with;
  • Advertising preferences and consent choices;
  • Performance, diagnostic, and interaction information processed by the advertising SDK.

Google determines the advertising services and ad selection mechanisms used for the App. We do not independently build advertising profiles or sell users' personal data. Google's processing of information for advertising is subject to Google's own terms and privacy policies, as well as the choices and settings available to users.

A consent message is presented when the App is first launched. The advertising options and processing that apply may depend on your location and the choices you make through that message. You may also be able to manage advertising preferences through the privacy options made available by Google or your device.

The App includes Apple's App Tracking Transparency integration. On supported Apple devices, the App may display Apple's system tracking permission request. If it is displayed, you can allow or deny tracking. Your choice affects whether the relevant tracking and advertising identifiers may be made available to advertising services. Denying permission does not prevent you from using the App's educational features.

3.5 App configuration and technical information

The App uses Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage, and Firebase Cloud Functions, to provide its functionality and make educational and competition-related information available.

The App may process basic technical information required to operate these services, such as app version, platform information, authentication status, service requests, and information needed to diagnose or protect the services. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may also process technical or diagnostic information as described above.

The App may store local settings and cached content using on-device storage. These items are used for App functionality and are not intended to form a personal user profile.

3.6 What we do not collect

The App does not request or intentionally collect for its ordinary educational functionality:

  • Your name, email address, telephone number, or personal account credentials;
  • Precise or approximate device location through location permissions;
  • Camera images, photos, or videos;
  • Microphone recordings or audio;
  • Contacts or address books;
  • Health or biometric information;
  • Financial or payment information;
  • Files already stored on your device, except that you may choose to save PDF documents provided by the App;
  • Personal academic records from a school account or university account.

The competition device identifier described in Section 3.3 and information processed by Google AdMob as described in Section 3.4 are exceptions to the general statement that the App does not process user-related identifiers.

We do not use Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics as separate analytics or crash-reporting products in the App.

We retain information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, taking into account the App's functionality and applicable legal requirements.
