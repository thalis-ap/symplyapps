1. What the App does

The App helps passengers know whether ticket inspectors are currently active in the area covered by the App, so that they can have their tickets ready for inspection and avoid queues in inspection areas. To provide this service, the App stores inspection-related records and sends push notifications.

2. Summary

  • We collect only what is needed to run the App: your sign-in identity, inspection records, and a push-notification token.
  • We do not collect your location, camera, photos, microphone, contacts, health, biometric, or financial data.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party trackers.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • You can request deletion of your account and data at any time by emailing symplyapp@gmail.com.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Account information

You can sign in using Google Sign-In, Sign in with Apple (on Apple devices), or Firebase anonymous sign-in. When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive and store your email address and display name, together with a unique user identifier generated by Firebase. If you use anonymous sign-in, we store only the anonymous user identifier and no email address or name.

3.2 Inspection records

The App stores records related to inspections that you submit or that are generated through your use of the App (for example, inspection events, timestamps, and related identifiers). These records are associated with your account identifier.

3.3 Push notifications

If you enable notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging provides us with a device registration token and platform information. We use this solely to deliver push notifications through Firebase Cloud Functions. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings; the token will no longer be used to deliver messages.

3.4 Basic technical data

Like most apps that use Firebase, basic technical data (such as app version, platform, and diagnostic information generated by the Firebase SDKs) may be processed to keep the service secure and working correctly.

3.5 What we do not collect

  • Precise or approximate location
  • Camera, photos, or media files
  • Microphone or audio
  • Contacts
  • Health, biometric, or financial information
  • Advertising identifiers or advertising data
  • Analytics, crash-reporting, or performance-monitoring data beyond what is strictly required to operate the Firebase services described above

4. How we use information and our legal bases

We use the information described above to:

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate you (performance of a contract);
  • Provide the App’s core feature — inspection information and inspection records (performance of a contract);
  • Send push notifications related to the service (performance of a contract or your consent, depending on your device settings);
  • Maintain the security, integrity, and proper functioning of the App (legitimate interests);
  • Comply with legal obligations (legal obligation).

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We share data only with the service providers needed to operate the App:

  • Google (Firebase / Google Cloud) — authentication, database (Cloud Firestore), push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging), and server functions (Cloud Functions), acting as our data processor.
  • Apple Inc. — only if you choose Sign in with Apple, to authenticate your sign-in.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the public.

6. International transfers

Firebase and Google Cloud process data in data centers that may be located outside Greece or the European Economic Area. Where this happens, transfers are carried out under appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses and Google’s data-processing terms.

7. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as needed to provide the App. Account data and associated inspection records are deleted when you request deletion (see section 8). We may retain limited records if we are legally required to do so, or to resolve disputes and enforce our agreements.

8. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and the personal data associated with it at any time. To do so, email symplyapp@gmail.com and tell us the email address associated with your account (or, if you used anonymous sign-in, the account identifier shown in the App). We will verify the request and delete your account and associated personal data promptly, and in any case within 30 days of verification.

Deletion removes your account information, inspection records linked to your account, and the push-notification token associated with your device. Data that we are required by law to keep will be retained only for as long as the law requires.

9. Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate data;
  • request deletion of your data;
  • request restriction of, or object to, processing;
  • request portability of your data;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Greece, the Hellenic Data Protection Authority, www.dpa.gr).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at symplyapp@gmail.com.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Data is transmitted using encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored in Firebase services that encrypt data at rest. Access to production data is restricted. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. If changes are significant, we will notify you in the App or by email before they take effect.
